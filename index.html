Smart Khata

Smart Khata is a simple and secure digital ledger application designed for shop owners, small businesses, and individuals to manage customer accounts and daily transactions.

Features

  • Manage customer and party accounts
  • Record Debit (DR) and Credit (CR) transactions
  • Automatic running balance calculation
  • Offline-first ledger using local device storage
  • Google account sign-in
  • Multi-device synchronization
  • Google Drive backup and restore
  • PDF account statements
  • CSV data export
  • English and Hindi language support
  • PIN and biometric app protection

Data & Backup

Smart Khata stores ledger information locally on the user's device. When cloud synchronization is enabled, account data may be synchronized using services associated with the user's authenticated Google account.

Smart Khata may also allow users to create backups in their Google Drive account. Drive access is requested only for application-specific backup data and is subject to the permissions granted by the user.

Privacy

Your business and ledger information remains associated with your account and is not sold to advertisers.
