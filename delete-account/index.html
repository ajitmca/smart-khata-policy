Smart Khata - Account & Data Deletion

Smart Khata users can request deletion of their account and associated application data by contacting the developer.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to guptaajit2009@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject: Smart Khata Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the Google email address associated with your Smart Khata account, so that we can verify the account.
  4. State clearly that you want your Smart Khata account and associated cloud data to be deleted.

Data that will be deleted

After the account deletion request is verified and processed, account-associated Smart Khata data under our control will be deleted, including applicable:

  • Smart Khata account/profile information
  • Shop or business profile information
  • Customer and supplier records
  • Ledger and transaction records
  • Synchronization-related cloud data
  • Device-related records associated with the Smart Khata account

Google Drive backups

Smart Khata may store backups in the application-specific data area of the user's Google Drive account. Users may also manage or remove Smart Khata backup data using the available backup-management functionality and applicable Google account controls.

Data stored on your device

Data stored locally on a user's device may be removed by clearing Smart Khata's application data or uninstalling the App, subject to Android platform behavior.

Data that may be retained

Certain limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations. Such information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process verified account and data deletion requests within 30 days. If additional time is required due to legal or technical reasons, we will communicate this where appropriate.
