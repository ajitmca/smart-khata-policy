Smart Khata - Data Deletion Request

Smart Khata users can request deletion of some or all of their Smart Khata data without deleting their Smart Khata account.

Delete data within the App

Smart Khata provides controls that allow users to manage and delete supported records directly from the App.

Depending on the type of data, users can delete supported:

  • Customer records
  • Supplier records
  • Ledger transactions
  • Receipt or bill attachments
  • Google Drive backup files through available backup controls

Deleting an individual record does not require deletion of the user's Smart Khata account.

Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to guptaajit2009@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject: Smart Khata Data Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the Google email address associated with your Smart Khata account.
  4. Clearly specify which Smart Khata data you would like to have deleted.

Data that can be requested for deletion

A deletion request may cover applicable:

  • Shop or business profile data
  • Customer and supplier information
  • Ledger and transaction records
  • Receipt or bill information
  • Cloud-synchronized Smart Khata data
  • Smart Khata Google Drive backup data

Data that may be retained

Certain limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Any such information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process verified data deletion requests within 30 days.
