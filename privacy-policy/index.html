Privacy Policy – Smart Khata

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Smart Khata ("the App") is a digital ledger application designed to help users manage customers, transactions, balances, reports, and business records.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Smart Khata.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use the App, you may enter information including:

  • Shop or business name
  • Owner name
  • Business address
  • Mobile number
  • GST information
  • Customer or party names
  • Customer mobile numbers and addresses
  • Ledger balances
  • Debit and credit transactions
  • Transaction particulars and notes
  • Reference or bill numbers
  • Payment modes
  • Receipt or bill images when attached by the user

2. Local Data Storage

Smart Khata is designed as an offline-first application. Ledger and business information is stored locally on your device so that core ledger functionality can continue to work without an internet connection.

3. Google Account

Smart Khata may allow you to sign in using your Google account. Google authentication is used to identify your account and enable features such as synchronization and backup.

The App does not receive or store your Google account password. Authentication is handled through Google's authentication services.

4. Cloud Synchronization

If cloud synchronization is enabled, Smart Khata may synchronize application data between your authorized devices using Google Firebase services such as Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

This may include customer, transaction, business profile, synchronization, and device-related information necessary to provide the synchronization feature.

5. Google Drive Backup

Smart Khata may request permission to access application-specific data in your Google Drive account for backup and restore functionality.

The App intends to use the Google Drive application-data permission (drive.appdata) so that it can access only data created for Smart Khata's backup functionality rather than requesting unrestricted access to your Google Drive files.

Google Drive backup access is used to create, locate, manage, and restore Smart Khata backup data associated with the Google account authorized by the user.

6. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to provide App functionality, including:

  • Maintaining customer ledgers
  • Calculating account balances
  • Displaying transaction history
  • Generating reports and statements
  • Synchronizing data between authorized devices
  • Creating and restoring backups
  • Providing application security and reliability

7. Data Sharing

Smart Khata does not sell your personal, customer, or ledger information to advertisers.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate features of the App, including Google services used for authentication, cloud synchronization, and backup.

8. Data Security

Smart Khata uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect application data. Depending on enabled features, these measures may include local application security, authentication, access controls, and secure connections to cloud services.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data remains on the user's device until it is deleted, the application data is cleared, or the application is removed, subject to Android platform behavior.

Cloud-synchronized or backed-up information may remain in the associated cloud account until it is deleted according to Smart Khata's available data-management functionality or the applicable Google service controls.

10. Permissions

Smart Khata may request permissions only when required for application features, including access necessary for selecting receipt images, authentication, notifications, and Google Drive backup authorization.

11. Children's Privacy

Smart Khata is intended as a business and ledger-management application and is not specifically directed toward children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Smart Khata's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or questions regarding your data, please contact the developer using the support email provided on the Smart Khata Google Play listing.
